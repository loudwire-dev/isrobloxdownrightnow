Why Roblox Is Up But Not Working

Even if Roblox servers are online, your device or connection may be causing issues.

  • Network restrictions or firewall blocking Roblox
  • Outdated Roblox client
  • Cache or browser issues
  • Weak Wi-Fi or high latency
Fix Roblox Not Working (Step-by-Step)
  • Restart your device
  • Update Roblox
  • Clear cache/browser data
  • Disable VPN
  • Check firewall settings
  • Try another network
  • Test another device
Common Roblox Errors
  • Error 267 – Server kick
  • Error 277 – Connection lost
  • Error 529 – Server issue
  • Error 610 – Connection failed
Improve Your Connection
  • Restart router
  • Use wired internet
  • Change DNS (1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8)
  • Close background apps
